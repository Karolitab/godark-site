Common questions

  • Apps will not lock. Go Dark needs Screen Time access. Open Go Dark, go to Settings, then Permissions, and turn on Screen Time.
  • My friend cannot join my board. Codes are letters and numbers only. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.
  • I want my data gone. Leave your board in Settings and delete the app. Board data is tied to a random ID, not to you.
